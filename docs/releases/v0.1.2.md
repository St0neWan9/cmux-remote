# CmuxRemote 0.1.2

Start new Claude or Codex conversations from your iPhone, plus faster and more
reliable connections. Install it on every Mac you share; the iPhone features
below need this version on the Mac.

## New: start a conversation from the phone

- On the iPhone, tap **+** in the conversation list (or next to a workspace),
  choose a folder from that workspace and pick Claude or Codex. cmux opens a new
  terminal tab in that workspace without taking focus on the Mac, starts the
  agent, and the phone opens the conversation once it appears.
- Folders are limited to the workspace's own project folders and their
  subfolders. The home folder, hidden folders and credential folders are never
  offered, and links that point outside the project are refused.
- This is a separate permission. When you approve an iPhone on the Mac, **Start
  new sessions** is listed with its own checkbox (on by default). iPhones paired
  before this version need to pair again to get it.
- The phone only ever starts `claude` or `codex`; it cannot run other commands.

## Connections

- The phone tries the local network and the relay at the same time and redials
  both immediately after a drop, so reconnecting is noticeably faster.
- A Bridge restart no longer needs a manual reconnect, and a relayed link that
  silently stops answering is redialled.
- Reading conversations costs less on the Mac when nothing has changed.

## Conversations

- Conversations are named after their cmux tab, background task notices are
  grouped with their replies, and a resumed Codex session is listed once.
- The Mac companion can show the Bridge log for troubleshooting.

## Install

Download `CmuxRemote-0.1.2.dmg`, quit CmuxRemote from the menu bar, then drag
the new copy to **Applications** and replace the old one. Saved pairings,
approved devices and sharing settings are kept.

## Requirements

- macOS 14 Sonoma or later
- The official cmux app
