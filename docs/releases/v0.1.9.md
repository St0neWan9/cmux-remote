# CmuxRemote 0.1.9

Both lists fold a Mac's older conversations under one row, so the list opens on what the reader is actually
working on.

## iPhone and Android

- **Older conversations fold.** A conversation stays in view while its Agent is running, while it waits on you,
  or while it was active in the last three days. The rest of each list sits under one row that names how many it
  holds — "earlier 21 conversations" — and tapping it opens them in place. The choice is remembered on the
  device, per list.
- **No list collapses to a bare heading.** Every workspace keeps its three newest conversations whatever their
  age, and time order keeps its six newest.
- **Search and the status filters never fold.** Looking for something shows every match.

Against the Mac these were measured on (71 conversations), the list opens on 19 rows and folds 52.

## Android

The Android build is 0.1.24 (`android-v0.1.24`); the Mac companion keeps its version.

## Install

Download `CmuxRemote-0.1.9.dmg`, open it and drag CmuxRemote to Applications. The app is Developer ID signed and
notarized for macOS 14 or later. This build is universal (Apple silicon and Intel).

CmuxRemote is not an official cmux product.
