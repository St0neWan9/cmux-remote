# CmuxRemote 0.1.5

Long conversations stay responsive. Opening a big transcript, pulling back
through it or watching a turn used to cost the Mac a second or more for every
page the phone read; the page the reader was on waited behind those reads.
Install this on every Mac you share — most of the fix is in the Mac app.

## Speed

- **The newest page parses a small window.** A page carries at most a few
  hundred messages, but the Bridge parsed two megabytes of the transcript for
  each one: about 0.7 s of CPU per read on a 78 MB record, every few seconds
  while a turn runs. It now parses 512 KB (730 ms → 88 ms measured), and pages
  the reader pulls keep the larger window and the messages that come with it.
- **The phone reads the Mac's session list far less often.** It used to read
  the whole list on every refresh — one to nine seconds on a Mac holding 52
  sessions, up to three times per refresh. It now reads it once every five
  seconds, and at once after this phone sends a prompt or stops a turn.
- **The Feed is read on its own schedule**, at most every eight seconds, and
  no longer twice per change.

## Fixed

- **A busy turn no longer loses messages.** When a turn recorded more than one
  page between two reads, the next read started above the messages on screen
  and the stretch in between was never fetched. It is now read from the page's
  own cursor, bounded to eight pages.

## Install

Download `CmuxRemote-0.1.5.dmg`, quit CmuxRemote from the menu bar, then drag
the new copy to **Applications**. The phone needs no new pairing; install the
matching phone build from TestFlight to get the client half of these changes.

## Requirements

- macOS 14 Sonoma or later
- The official cmux app
